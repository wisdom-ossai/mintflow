Privacy Policy

Mintflow · Last updated November 21, 2026

Mintflow (“we”, “us”) helps you understand your money. We collect only what we need to run the app, and we never move money or hold your bank login credentials.

Data we collect

  • Account: email, name, authentication identifiers (including Sign in with Apple / Google).
  • Financial: transactions, budgets, goals, bills, accounts, and insights you create or sync via Plaid.
  • Device: push notification token, app usage needed for core features.
  • Purchases: subscription status via Apple/Google and RevenueCat (no full payment card numbers).

How we use data

To provide budgeting, categorization, insights, reminders, subscriptions, and support. AI features send anonymized merchant/spend summaries to our processor (Anthropic) — not your bank credentials.

Bank connections

Bank credentials stay with Plaid. Mintflow is read-only: we cannot transfer funds or change your bank accounts.

Receipt scans

Receipt photos are processed to extract merchant and amount, then discarded. We store the resulting transaction fields, not the image.

Sharing

We use processors who help us operate the service (hosting, auth, bank aggregation, AI, push, analytics, subscriptions). We do not sell your personal data.

Retention & deletion

You can delete your account in the app (Privacy & security). Deletion removes your Mintflow data and revokes linked Plaid items where applicable.
